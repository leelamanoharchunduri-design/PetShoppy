OUR STORY

A Happy Place for Every Pet Parent

PetShoppy was created with one simple idea: caring for pets should be joyful, convenient and accessible. We understand that pets are more than animals – they are beloved members of our families.

From everyday essentials to fun toys, nutritious food, grooming accessories and useful care products, we aim to make it easier for pet parents to find what their companions need.

Whether you are welcoming your first puppy, caring for a playful kitten or looking after an older companion, we want to support you at every stage of their journey.

WHAT DRIVES US

Our Mission & Our Vision

Building a world where every pet receives love, attention and responsible care.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make quality pet care simple and accessible for every pet parent. We aim to provide carefully selected pet food, accessories, toys, grooming essentials and everyday supplies that support the comfort and well-being of pets.

We also encourage responsible pet ownership by sharing helpful information about nutrition, hygiene, exercise, training and regular veterinary checkups.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted destination for pet parents by creating a welcoming shopping experience that puts pets first.

We aspire to build a caring pet community where responsible adoption, animal welfare, informed pet care and access to reliable products and professional veterinary guidance are encouraged.

VETERINARY & WELLNESS

Healthy Pets Start with Responsible Care

Your pet's health is an important part of their happiness. Regular veterinary visits help monitor their overall well-being and identify health concerns early.

Regular Health Checkups Schedule routine examinations with a qualified veterinarian.
Vaccination & Prevention Follow a veterinarian-recommended vaccination and parasite prevention schedule.
Health Monitoring Pay attention to changes in appetite, behaviour, activity and general health.
Professional Guidance Consult qualified veterinary professionals for medical advice, diagnosis and treatment.

PetShoppy shares general pet-care information. It does not replace professional veterinary diagnosis or treatment.
