Golden Retriever

Friendly Hearts. Playful Personalities.

Golden Retrievers are affectionate, intelligent and sociable dogs. They generally enjoy companionship, outdoor activities and interactive games.

Golden Retriever Characteristics

SizeMedium to Large
Weight24–34 kg (approx.)
Height55–61 cm (approx.)
Life Expectancy10–12 years (approx.)
CoatLong Double Coat
SheddingHigh

Personality

Friendly, affectionate, intelligent, playful and generally sociable.

Adaptability

Benefits from companionship, comfortable resting space and regular outdoor activities.

Diet & Nutrition

Needs complete and balanced food suitable for its age, activity and health requirements. Monitor portions with veterinary guidance.

Training & Exercise

Enjoys walking, fetching, interactive games and reward-based training.

Grooming

Regular brushing, appropriate bathing, ear checks, dental care and nail maintenance are important.

Health Considerations

May be predisposed to hip and elbow dysplasia, certain heart conditions and skin problems. Regular veterinary check-ups are recommended.

Are Golden Retrievers friendly?

They are generally known for their friendly and affectionate temperament.

Do Golden Retrievers need grooming?

Yes. Their double coat benefits from regular brushing and routine grooming.

Labrador Retriever

Energetic. Loyal. Loving.

Labradors are popular companion dogs known for their friendly nature, enthusiasm and willingness to learn.

Labrador Characteristics

SizeMedium to Large
Weight25–36 kg (approx.)
Height54–62 cm (approx.)
Life Expectancy10–12 years (approx.)
CoatShort Double Coat
SheddingModerate to High

Personality

Generally friendly, outgoing, playful and people-oriented.

Adaptability

Needs regular activity, companionship and an environment suited to its energy level.

Diet & Nutrition

Needs balanced nutrition and appropriate portion management. Monitor body condition with veterinary advice.

Training & Exercise

Enjoys walks, retrieving games, swimming where safe and positive reinforcement training.

Grooming

Regular brushing, occasional bathing, nail care, dental hygiene and ear checks.

Health Considerations

May be predisposed to joint problems, obesity and certain eye conditions. Routine veterinary care is important.

Are Labradors good family companions?

They are often sociable and affectionate, although every dog needs suitable training and supervision.

Are Labradors active?

Yes. They generally benefit from regular walks, play and mental stimulation.

Beagle

Curious. Cheerful. Adventurous.

Beagles are small to medium-sized scent hounds known for their curiosity, distinctive ears and interest in exploring scents.

Beagle Characteristics

SizeSmall to Medium
Weight9–14 kg (approx.)
Height33–41 cm (approx.)
Life Expectancy12–15 years (approx.)
CoatShort Coat
SheddingModerate

Personality

Curious, friendly, energetic and often very interested in scents.

Adaptability

Benefits from secure outdoor spaces, companionship and supervised exploration.

Diet & Nutrition

Needs balanced meals and portion control appropriate to age and activity.

Training & Exercise

Scent games, walks and consistent reward-based training can be helpful.

Grooming

Regular brushing, ear checks, dental care and nail maintenance.

Health Considerations

May be predisposed to ear infections, obesity and certain joint or eye conditions.

Are Beagles curious dogs?

Yes. Their scent-hound background makes them especially interested in following smells.

Do Beagles need exercise?

They generally benefit from regular walks, play and supervised scent-based activities.

Pug

Charming. Affectionate. Playful.

Pugs are small companion dogs recognised for their compact build, expressive faces and affectionate nature.

Pug Characteristics

SizeSmall
Weight6–8 kg (approx.)
Height25–33 cm (approx.)
Life Expectancy12–15 years (approx.)
CoatShort Coat
SheddingModerate to High

Personality

Often affectionate, sociable, playful and fond of companionship.

Adaptability

Can adapt to smaller homes, but needs a comfortable, cool environment.

Diet & Nutrition

Needs balanced food and appropriate portions. Monitor body condition with veterinary guidance.

Training & Exercise

Short, gentle activities and positive reinforcement are generally suitable. Avoid strenuous activity in hot weather.

Grooming

Regular brushing, careful cleaning of facial folds, dental care and nail maintenance.

Health Considerations

Pugs can experience breathing difficulties, eye problems and heat sensitivity because of their flat facial structure. Veterinary care is especially important.

Are Pugs suitable for apartment living?

They can adapt to apartment environments when their comfort, activity and care needs are met.

Do Pugs tolerate hot weather well?

No. Their breathing structure makes heat management particularly important. Keep them cool and seek veterinary advice about exercise.

German Shepherd

Intelligent. Confident. Devoted.

German Shepherds are intelligent working dogs known for their trainability, alertness and strong bonds with their families.

German Shepherd Characteristics

SizeLarge
Weight22–40 kg (approx.)
Height55–65 cm (approx.)
Life Expectancy9–13 years (approx.)
CoatDouble Coat
SheddingHigh

Personality

Often intelligent, loyal, alert and responsive to structured training.

Adaptability

Needs space for activity, companionship, socialisation and consistent routines.

Diet & Nutrition

Benefits from complete, balanced nutrition suitable for its life stage and activity.

Training & Exercise

Enjoys structured training, walks, puzzle activities and appropriate physical exercise.

Grooming

Regular brushing, especially during seasonal shedding, plus dental and nail care.

Health Considerations

May be predisposed to hip and elbow dysplasia, digestive conditions and certain spinal problems.

Are German Shepherds intelligent?

They are widely recognised for their intelligence and ability to learn through consistent training.

Do German Shepherds need regular activity?

Yes. They generally need physical exercise and mental stimulation suited to their age and health.

Small Dog Breeds

Little Companions. Big Personalities.

Small dogs include many different breeds with unique personalities, coat types and care needs. Their requirements vary by individual breed.

Small Dog Characteristics

SizeSmall
WeightVaries by breed
HeightVaries by breed
Life ExpectancyVaries by breed
CoatShort, Medium or Long
SheddingVaries by breed

Personality

Can be affectionate, energetic, independent, playful or alert depending on breed.

Adaptability

Many can adapt to smaller homes, provided their activity and enrichment needs are met.

Diet & Nutrition

Choose balanced food suitable for the specific breed, age and size.

Training & Exercise

Provide suitable walks, playtime, socialisation and positive training.

Grooming

Grooming requirements depend on coat length, texture and breed.

Health Considerations

Health risks differ greatly between breeds. Ask a veterinarian about breed-specific screening and care.

Are small dogs suitable for apartments?

Many can adapt to apartment living, but they still need exercise, training and enrichment.

Do all small dogs have the same care requirements?

No. Diet, grooming, exercise and health needs vary significantly between breeds.
