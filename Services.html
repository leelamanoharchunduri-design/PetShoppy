NEED MORE INFORMATION?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our pet care services.

How can I book a pet care service?

Choose a service, complete the appointment form with your pet's details and preferred schedule, and submit your request. The team can contact you to confirm availability.

Can I book grooming services for both dogs and cats?

You can submit a grooming enquiry for dogs or cats. The available grooming options will depend on your pet's needs and the participating service provider.

Are veterinary consultations available?

Veterinary consultation requests may be coordinated with participating veterinary professionals. Diagnosis and treatment must be handled by a qualified veterinarian.

Can I request an at-home service?

Yes. Select At-Home Pet Care in the booking form and include your location and requirements in the additional information field. Availability depends on the service area.

Are the prices shown on this page final?

No. Displayed prices are illustrative starting rates. Final quotations depend on the service, pet size, location, duration and provider.

How will I know if my appointment is confirmed?

Submitting the form sends an appointment request. Your appointment is confirmed only after the team contacts you and verifies the details.
