Persian Cat

Gentle Nature. Beautiful Long Coat.

Persian cats are known for their long, luxurious coats, round faces and calm personalities. They generally enjoy peaceful environments and comfortable indoor living.

Persian Cat Characteristics

Size Medium to Large
Weight Approximately 3–6 kg
Life Expectancy Approximately 12–17 years
Coat Long and Dense
Personality Calm and Affectionate
Grooming Daily Brushing

Personality

Persian cats are generally gentle, affectionate and relaxed. They often appreciate quiet surroundings and comfortable resting places.

Nutrition

Provide balanced cat food suitable for their age and activity level. Fresh drinking water should always be available.

Grooming

Their long coat needs regular brushing to prevent tangles and matting. Keep their eyes and facial area clean.

Exercise

Encourage gentle play with interactive toys, climbing structures and short play sessions throughout the day.

Living Environment

A clean, comfortable indoor environment with quiet resting areas is generally suitable for Persian cats.

Health Care

Ask a veterinarian about eye care, breathing concerns, dental hygiene and breed-related health screening.

Are Persian cats suitable for indoor living?

Yes. Persian cats generally adapt well to indoor environments when provided with enrichment, attention and comfortable spaces.

Do Persian cats need regular grooming?

Yes. Their long coats require frequent brushing to reduce tangles and maintain coat cleanliness.

Siamese Cat

Social Nature. Curious Personality.

Siamese cats are recognised for their colour-point coats, blue eyes and vocal personalities. They are often active, intelligent and interested in interacting with people.

Siamese Cat Characteristics

Size Medium
Weight Approximately 2.5–5.5 kg
Life Expectancy Approximately 12–20 years
Coat Short and Fine
Personality Social and Vocal
Grooming Weekly Brushing

Personality

Siamese cats are often sociable, curious and communicative. They enjoy interaction and companionship.

Nutrition

Provide complete and balanced cat food. Monitor portions and maintain a consistent feeding routine.

Grooming

Their short coat generally needs occasional brushing. Regular nail trimming and dental care are useful.

Exercise

Offer puzzle toys, climbing spaces, interactive games and activities that encourage exploration.

Living Environment

Siamese cats benefit from a stimulating indoor environment with companionship and opportunities for play.

Health Care

Schedule regular veterinary examinations. Discuss dental health and inherited conditions with your veterinarian.

Are Siamese cats active?

They are generally energetic and enjoy interactive play, climbing and exploring.

Do Siamese cats enjoy companionship?

Many Siamese cats enjoy spending time with people and may seek regular interaction.

Maine Coon

Big Personality. Gentle Companion.

Maine Coons are one of the largest domestic cat breeds. They are known for their long coats, bushy tails, intelligence and friendly nature.

Maine Coon Characteristics

Size Large
Weight Approximately 4–8 kg
Life Expectancy Approximately 12–15 years
Coat Long and Thick
Personality Friendly and Intelligent
Grooming Regular Brushing

Personality

Maine Coons are often friendly, intelligent and playful. Many enjoy spending time with their families.

Nutrition

Offer balanced food appropriate for their size, age and activity. Monitor feeding portions.

Grooming

Their thick coat needs regular brushing, especially around the underside and areas prone to tangles.

Exercise

Provide sturdy cat trees, interactive toys and opportunities for climbing and exploration.

Living Environment

Give them enough room to move, comfortable resting areas and appropriately sized accessories.

Health Care

Discuss screening for heart and joint conditions with a qualified veterinarian.

Are Maine Coons large cats?

Yes. They are a large domestic cat breed and often have a substantial build.

Do Maine Coons need grooming?

Yes. Regular brushing helps keep their long coat clean and free from tangles.

Bengal Cat

Wild Looks. Playful Energy.

Bengal cats are known for their distinctive spotted or marbled coats. They are generally active, curious and enjoy interactive activities.

Bengal Cat Characteristics

Size Medium to Large
Weight Approximately 3.5–7 kg
Life Expectancy Approximately 12–16 years
Coat Short and Patterned
Personality Active and Curious
Grooming Occasional Brushing

Personality

Bengal cats are often curious, confident and energetic. They enjoy exploration and interactive play.

Nutrition

Feed a complete and balanced diet appropriate for their life stage. Provide fresh drinking water.

Grooming

Their short coat is relatively easy to maintain. Include regular nail and dental care.

Exercise

Offer climbing structures, puzzle feeders and interactive toys to support their active nature.

Living Environment

Provide a secure and enriching environment with suitable climbing and play opportunities.

Health Care

Ask your veterinarian about appropriate breed-related health screening and routine checkups.

Are Bengal cats playful?

Yes. They are generally active cats that enjoy games, climbing and environmental enrichment.

Do Bengal cats need much grooming?

Their short coat usually requires less brushing than long-haired breeds.

British Shorthair

Calm Nature. Charming Appearance.

British Shorthair cats are recognised for their round faces, dense coats and sturdy appearance. They are often calm and independent.

British Shorthair Characteristics

Size Medium to Large
Weight Approximately 3–8 kg
Life Expectancy Approximately 12–20 years
Coat Short and Dense
Personality Calm and Independent
Grooming Weekly Brushing

Personality

British Shorthairs are often relaxed, affectionate and independent. They generally appreciate routine.

Nutrition

Provide balanced meals and monitor portions to support a healthy body condition.

Grooming

Brush their dense coat regularly and maintain routine nail and dental care.

Exercise

Encourage daily play using toys, climbing areas and short interactive activities.

Living Environment

A calm indoor space with resting areas, scratching posts and toys can suit their lifestyle.

Health Care

Ask your veterinarian about heart screening, weight management and dental health.

Are British Shorthair cats independent?

They are often comfortable spending some time independently while still enjoying companionship.

Do British Shorthairs need brushing?

Yes. Regular brushing helps maintain their dense coat and remove loose hair.

Ragdoll Cat

Gentle Companion. Beautiful Blue Eyes.

Ragdolls are known for their striking blue eyes, soft coats and affectionate personalities. They often enjoy companionship and relaxed play.

Ragdoll Cat Characteristics

Size Large
Weight Approximately 4–9 kg
Life Expectancy Approximately 12–17 years
Coat Semi-long and Silky
Personality Gentle and Affectionate
Grooming Regular Brushing

Personality

Ragdolls are often friendly, gentle and people-oriented. Many enjoy relaxed companionship.

Nutrition

Provide balanced cat food that meets their nutritional needs. Monitor portions and activity.

Grooming

Brush their semi-long coat regularly to remove loose hair and reduce tangles.

Exercise

Encourage gentle play, interactive toys and suitable climbing opportunities.

Living Environment

Create a safe indoor environment with comfortable sleeping spaces and daily companionship.

Health Care

Discuss heart health, dental care and appropriate genetic screening with your veterinarian.

Are Ragdoll cats affectionate?

Many Ragdolls enjoy human companionship and are known for their gentle nature.

Do Ragdolls need grooming?

Yes. Regular brushing helps maintain their semi-long coat.

CARE THAT MAKES A DIFFERENCE

Happy Cats Start with Loving Care

From healthy nutrition to grooming, playtime and comfortable surroundings, discover simple ways to support your cat's everyday well-being.

Healthy Nutrition

Offer complete and balanced cat food, fresh water and suitable feeding portions.

Grooming & Hygiene

Maintain coat cleanliness, nail care, dental hygiene and a clean litter box.

Play & Enrichment

Use interactive toys, scratching posts and climbing spaces to encourage activity.

Comfortable Living

Provide safe resting places, clean surroundings and a calm home environment.

VETERINARY & CAT WELLNESS

Keep Your Feline Friend Healthy

Regular veterinary care is an important part of responsible cat ownership. Professional checkups help monitor your cat's health at every life stage.

Routine Health Checkups Schedule regular examinations with a qualified veterinarian.
Vaccination & Prevention Follow professional advice about vaccinations and parasite prevention.
Dental Care Maintain oral hygiene and discuss dental examinations with your vet.
Health Monitoring Monitor appetite, behaviour, litter habits and activity for changes.
